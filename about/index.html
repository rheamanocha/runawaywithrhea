About

Hi, I’m Rhea

I’m a travel content creator based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I have an insatiable wanderlust, a love for active adventures, and a habit of sharing recommendations with anyone who will listen.

My travel philosophy

01

Consider your impact

Tourism can strain the places we love, from locals priced out of their homes to mistreated trek porters. Learn the local customs, follow leave no trace, and think about your impact as you plan.

02

Plan, but embrace spontaneity

I’m a planner at heart, but I always leave unstructured time for what I find along the way: a secret swim spot, a yoga class on the beach, a night out before our flight home.

03

Be curious and open

Get out of your comfort zone. Try the local cuisine, take on new activities, and say yes to the things that scare but excite you.

Some recent favorite travel memories

Canoeing in the Grand TetonsCanoeing across the pristinely clear Jenny Lake while the Grand Tetons towered above us. We drifted around the lake, taking in the grandeur on a picturesque summer day.
Exploring the Puerto Rican rainforestEl Yunque is full of biodiversity, waterfalls, and my favorite: swim spots. The cool water was a perfect reprieve from the rainforest humidity, and I had the best time splashing around with friends.
Biking along Lake KawaguchiLake Kawaguchi sits in the foothills of Mount Fuji, and an e-bike makes the loop around it easy. If you get lucky, you’ll catch glimpses of Fuji along the way.
