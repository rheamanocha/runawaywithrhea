Overview

Less than an hour from Santa Barbara, Ojai feels like a secret the rest of California hasn’t figured out yet. It’s a small town of orange groves, jacaranda trees, and mountains that glow pink at sunset, and every corner has something tucked away to find.

We spent three days here in June, and these are the gems worth seeking out, followed by where we stayed and where we ate.

Hidden gems around town

Bart’s Books

My favorite find. It’s an outdoor bookstore where the shelves line the sidewalk and spill into a shaded courtyard with tables for reading. Give yourself time to wander.

Rhea 400Bart’s Books

Heavenly Honey Company

Taste your way down a counter of honey pumps, from wildflower and fiddleneck to sage and Ojai lavender. Pick up a jar and some tea on your way out.

Walk the town

The shops and arcade are easy to cover on foot. Stop at the bakery counter for sourdough and pastries.

Where we stayed

Where we stayed: Ojai East End Chic Ranch Home

We booked an east end ranch home on Airbnb with big windows, a wall of books, and a macramé hanging chair I claimed right away. Woven lanterns hung in the garden, and a hand-glazed “Pixie Palace” sign hung on the door.
