Everything to eat in Lima

01 / 14

Local coffee and Museo del Café Peruano

Peruvian coffee is grown in the highlands, and the museum walks through it from bean to cup. Order an iced coffee and look at the beans up close.

02 / 14

Empanadas

Baked pastry pockets with a savory filling. This one was stuffed with shredded chicken and came with a lime wedge.

03 / 14

Chifa

Peru’s Chinese-Peruvian cooking, and a whole genre of its own in Lima. Wontons, dumplings, and wok dishes made with Peruvian ingredients.

04 / 14

Choco Museo

A small museum and shop for Peruvian cacao. The wall map follows the pod from harvest to roasting, grinding, and chocolate.

05 / 14

Choclo con queso

Choclo is the Andean corn with huge, chewy kernels. Street vendors serve it boiled with a slab of fresh, salty cheese.

06 / 14

Papa rellena

A mashed potato shell stuffed with seasoned filling, then fried until golden. A classic street snack.

07 / 14

Inca Kola

Peru’s fluorescent yellow soda, sweet and a little like bubblegum. It is everywhere, and it goes with just about everything.

08 / 14

Plantain chips and corn nuts

Chifles are crisp fried plantain slices. Cancha is toasted corn kernels, salty and crunchy. Both show up next to ceviche.

09 / 14

Ceviche

Raw fish cured in lime juice with onion and chili. Peru’s national dish, served cold and best eaten at lunch.

10 / 14

Jalea mixta

A pile of fried seafood under a heap of pickled red onion, with crispy plantain chips on the side. Made to share.

11 / 14

Local fruits

The markets are stacked with fruit you rarely see at home, including cherimoya, passion fruit, and lucuma. Buy a few and taste your way through.

12 / 14

Lucuma smoothie

Lucuma is a Peruvian fruit with a creamy, caramel-like flavor, and it is blended into smoothies and ice cream. Easy to find at the markets.

13 / 14

Picarones

Ring-shaped fritters made from squash and sweet potato, fried to order and served with a sweet syrup.

14 / 14

Turkey sandwich

A warm roll piled with sliced roast turkey and onions. A simple Lima lunch, and one of the best sandwiches on the trip.
