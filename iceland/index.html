Tips if you go

  • Bring really good layers and warm accessories. I loved my gear from Halfdays. Make sure it’s really waterproof in case of rain, and expect strong wind.
  • You don’t really need proper snow boots, but a decent lightweight waterproof hiking boot helps. I wore my Timberlands.
  • Snaefellsnes can get extremely windy, so be careful opening your car door.
  • Decide whether to drive the ring road or stay in Reykjavik and take short trips. Tours are available.
  • Stop at grocery stores when you can if you’re driving the ring road. Food can be expensive, and most stops are heavier on meat and seafood.
  • Northern lights season is fall and winter, and puffins are seasonal.
  • Get the gas station fries and put the spice on top.
